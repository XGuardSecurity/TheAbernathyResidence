Questions

What families ask us

Who can live at The Abernathy Residence?

Adults 18 and older, including seniors and adults with disabilities, who need help with daily living but do not need continuous nursing or medical care. A physical exam within 30 days before moving in is required.

Do you provide nursing care?

Yes. We provide licensed nurses for our residents: an RN and an LPN from our licensed nursing partner, who visit residents in the home. Residents keep their own doctors, and we help arrange every appointment.

How do residents pay?

We accept private pay only; we do not accept Medicaid or other government-funded placements. Residents pay from their own income or savings, with help from family, or with long-term care insurance alongside private funds. Request a private consultation and our administrator will walk you through the arrangements.

Can family and friends visit?

Yes. Visiting hours are set together with each resident, and family may visit at any hour when a resident is seriously ill. Visits can be in the resident's room, the second living room or outdoors.

Why don't you list prices?

Every person's needs are different, so charges are shared privately with the resident or their representative after a free care assessment, along with the written terms. Speak with our administrator to request a private consultation.
