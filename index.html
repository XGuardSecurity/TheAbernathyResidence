Questions

What families ask us

Who can live at The Abernathy Residence?

Adults 18 and older, including seniors and adults with disabilities, who need help with daily living but do not need continuous nursing or medical care. A physical exam within 30 days before moving in is required.

Do you provide nursing care?

Nursing is provided by an independent licensed nursing partner with an RN and an LPN, who can visit residents in the home. Residents may also choose their own doctors, nurses, therapists or hospice. We help arrange every visit.

How do residents pay?

Most residents pay privately, from savings, retirement income or long-term care insurance. Veterans and surviving spouses may qualify for VA Aid and Attendance benefits to help with the cost. Ask us and we can point you to the right place to apply.

Can family and friends visit?

Yes. Visiting hours are set together with each resident, and family may visit at any hour when a resident is seriously ill. Visits can be in the resident's room, the second living room or outdoors.

Why don't you list prices?

Every person's needs are different, so each plan is priced for the person after a free care assessment. Call us and we will give you a clear written quote.
